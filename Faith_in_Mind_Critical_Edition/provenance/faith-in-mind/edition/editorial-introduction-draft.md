# Editorial Introduction Draft: Faith in Mind

Date: 2026-05-08
Status: first draft

## 1. Work and Aim

This edition presents a poem-first critical text of the `信心銘` (`Faith in Mind`, `Xinxin Ming`, `Shinjinmei`), traditionally attributed to Sengcan (`僧璨`, d. 606). The aim of the present package is deliberately narrower than a full edition of the later commentary tradition. It is to establish a defensible critical reading text of the poem itself from the witness set now assembled and stabilized in this repository.

The project did not begin in that narrow form. The package first grew outward through OCR, manual correction, comparison, commentary controls, and bounded corroborative searches because the base `T1` witness survives inside a commentary-dominant environment and initially had to be stabilized locally before the poem spine could be separated confidently from surrounding prose. That work is now complete enough that the project can and should contract again to its real object: the poem.

## 2. Witness Basis

The present reading text is based on `T1`, the strongest direct standalone witness available in the package, used as the locked starting spine. The edition does not treat every later or cleaner witness as automatically superior. Instead, `T1` remains authoritative unless direct image review or bounded comparison support already logged in the package justifies a departure from raw `T1`.

Comparison witnesses `T4`, `T5`, `T2`, `T3`, `A1`, `A2`, and `A3` were used primarily to:

- confirm the transmitted order of the poem,
- support a small number of repaired loci,
- clarify the closing branch,
- and distinguish genuine poem absence from commentary contamination or local OCR noise.

Commentary and reception witnesses were also assembled in depth, but in this edition they function as a secondary scholarly track. They are preserved because they help explain reception history, witness ecology, and branch support, yet they do not determine the main poem text.

## 3. Editorial Method

The edition follows a best-witness critical model rather than a fully eclectic reconstruction. Its working rules are simple:

1. Keep `T1` as the base witness.
2. Emend only where the package already contains sufficient direct image or comparison evidence.
3. Record supplied or remapped loci explicitly instead of smoothing them silently into the text.
4. Preserve omission judgments where the broader tradition is fuller than the surviving `T1` witness.
5. Exclude commentary prose from the published poem text even where it was necessary during recovery.

This approach matters most at a few loci where the package had to make explicit editorial decisions:

- `T1-p021.l01a = 一種不通兩處失功` is carried as a supplied comparison-supported locus.
- `T1-p031.l01 = 縱有是非紛然失心` reflects a later image-led correction over an earlier OCR-driven opening graph.
- `T1-p036.l05 = 一空同兩齊含萬象` is preserved at its corrected local position after the earlier `l01` placement was shown to be a commentary-column misassignment.
- `T1-p047.l01` and `T1-p065.l01` are accepted comparison-supported repairs.
- `T1-p077.l01a = 但能如是何慮不畢` is carried as a supplied closing locus from comparison support.

The most important negative decision concerns the close. The broader transmitted tradition preserves `若不如此必不須守` before `若如是必不須守`, and several comparison witnesses support that fuller branch. But the corresponding `T1` locus is not a clean poem line: it is commentary material. The edition therefore preserves an omission judgment rather than silently reimporting the fuller traditional line into the `T1` reading text.

## 4. Scope and Secondary Material

The package now preserves two distinct layers:

- the main poem-first critical edition,
- and a parallel secondary archive of commentary, reception, and corroborative work.

This separation is not cosmetic. It marks a genuine change in editorial scope. The main edition should no longer be delayed by unresolved commentary scraps. Four residual fragment walls remain documented in the package:

- `T1-p007.l03`
- `T1-p007.l12`
- `T1-p012.l02`
- `T1-p029.l06`

These are no longer treated as hidden poem blockers. They are fragmentary commentary residue on the present evidence basis.

## 5. Present State of the Edition

The text published here is therefore not a diplomatic transcription of `T1`, nor an unrestricted eclectic reconstruction. It is a poem-first critical reading text built from a corrected `T1` spine, checked against the locked witness set, and accompanied by a concise apparatus for the loci where the editorial decisions materially matter.

The next stage of work is no longer rescue-oriented. It is presentational: refining the TEI, tightening apparatus language, and shaping this draft introduction into a final editorial preface.
