# Editorial Introduction: Faith in Mind

Date: 2026-05-08
Status: publication preface

## 1. Work and Aim

This edition presents a poem-first critical text of the `信心銘` (`Faith in Mind`, `Xinxin Ming`, `Shinjinmei`), traditionally attributed to Sengcan (`僧璨`, d. 606). Its immediate aim is not to edit the full later commentary tradition, but to establish a defensible critical reading text of the poem itself from the witness set assembled and stabilized in this package.

The poem survives in more than one textual environment. Some witnesses transmit it as a relatively direct poem text, while others preserve it in anthology, commentary, or reception settings. For that reason, the package had to document not only the poem witnesses themselves, but also the broader textual ecology in which the poem was copied, quoted, and explained. The present edition uses that larger body of work as support, but its main object remains the poem.

## 2. Witness History and Basis of Text

The reading text is built from `T1`, the strongest direct standalone witness available in the package, and uses `T1` as the locked starting spine. This does not mean that `T1` is treated as mechanically infallible. It means that the edition begins from the base witness actually in hand and departs from it only where direct image review or already logged comparison evidence justifies doing so.

The primary comparison controls are `T4`, `T5`, `T2`, `T3`, `A1`, `A2`, and `A3`. Together they confirm the transmitted order of the poem, support a small number of repaired or supplied loci, clarify the shape of the closing branch, and help distinguish genuine poem absence from commentary intrusion or local OCR noise. Commentary and reception witnesses were also collected in depth, but they are used here as secondary scholarly controls rather than coequal reading-text authorities.

In short, this is a best-witness critical edition grounded in a corrected `T1` poem spine and disciplined by a locked comparison set, not a free eclectic reconstruction from the whole later tradition.

## 3. Editorial Principles

The edition follows a small set of explicit rules.

1. `T1` remains the base witness.
2. A corrected reading is accepted only where direct image evidence or logged comparison support is already sufficient.
3. Supplied or remapped loci are recorded explicitly rather than normalized silently into the text.
4. Commentary prose is excluded from the published poem body even where it was useful during recovery.
5. Omission judgments are preserved where the broader tradition is fuller than the surviving `T1` poem witness.

These principles matter most at a limited number of poem loci. The edition therefore carries a concise apparatus rather than an inflated record of every workflow trace. Among the most important interventions are the supplied loci at `T1-p021.l01a` and `T1-p077.l01a`, the corrected image-led reading at `T1-p031.l01`, the remapped poem locus at `T1-p036.l05`, and the preserved omission judgment before `T1-p076`.

The negative decision at the close is especially important. Several comparison witnesses preserve `若不如此必不須守` before `若如是必不須守`, but the corresponding `T1` location does not preserve a clean poem line. The edition therefore records the broader transmitted line as an omission judgment rather than silently restoring it into the base witness.

## 4. Scope and Secondary Material

This package now preserves two clearly separated layers:

- the main poem-first critical edition,
- and a secondary archive of commentary, reception, corroborative, and process material.

That secondary layer remains valuable. It documents witness ecology, reception history, and the evidentiary basis for several poem-level decisions. It also preserves the limits of the surviving material honestly. But it does not govern the main poem text, and unresolved commentary fragments are no longer treated as hidden blockers for the edition of the poem.

At the close of the recovery phase, four residual loci remain unresolved on the present evidence basis:

- `T1-p007.l03`
- `T1-p007.l12`
- `T1-p012.l02`
- `T1-p029.l06`

These are preserved in the package as fragment walls. They do not alter the published poem text.

## 5. Present Edition

The text published here is therefore neither a diplomatic transcription of `T1` nor an unrestricted eclectic reconstruction. It is a poem-first critical reading text built from a corrected `T1` spine, checked against a locked witness set, and accompanied by a restrained apparatus at the loci where editorial decisions materially affect the reading.

The commentary and reception archive remains part of the scholarly value of the package, but the edition itself is now in publication form: a TEI poem text, a machine-readable apparatus, a documented editorial method, and the supporting witness and process record needed to explain how the text was made.
