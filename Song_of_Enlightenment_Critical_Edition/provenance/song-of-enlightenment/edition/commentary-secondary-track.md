# Commentary Secondary Track: Song of Enlightenment

Date: 2026-05-15

## Status

Commentary, reception, and derivative anthology material remain secondary in this edition.

## Rule

- Exact poem witnesses control the main reading text.
- Close exact controls and strong anthology controls may reinforce boundary or witness-class judgments.
- Commentary does not become a parallel main workstream.
- Japanese commentary is ignored for reader-facing editorial use in this package.

## What this track is still for

- witness ecology
- packaging and boundary clarification
- later intertextual or quotation study
- future commentary or reception work outside the core poem edition

## What this track is not for

- silent normalization of the poem
- hidden copy-text replacement
- broad explanatory expansion inside the main reading text
- forcing late commentary material into ordinary line-by-line collation

## Current consequence

The publication-facing Song edition remains poem-first, with commentary and reception material preserved but clearly subordinate.
