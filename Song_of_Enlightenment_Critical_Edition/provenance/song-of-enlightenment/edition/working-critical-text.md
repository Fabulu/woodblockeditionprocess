# Working Critical Text: Song of Enlightenment

Date: `2026-05-15`
Status: `active`

This is the current stabilized poem-first reading text extracted from the synchronized Chinese and English frontier in `translation-diff-log.md`. It reflects the accepted grouped poem loci carried into the edition package, not a fresh full variant collation.

1. `君不見 絕學無爲閑道人 不除妄想不求真 無明實性即佛性 幻化空身即法身`  
   English: Do you not see? The person of the Way, at ease in non-doing and beyond learning, does not cut off delusive thoughts and does not seek the true. The actual nature of ignorance is itself buddha-nature; this illusory, empty body is itself the dharma-body.  
   Locus: `opening-shared-a`
2. `法身覺了無一物 本源自性天真佛 五蘊浮雲空去來 三毒水泡虛出沒`  
   English: When the dharma-body is awakened to, not a single thing remains. In its original source, self-nature is the innately real buddha. The five aggregates are like drifting clouds, empty in their coming and going; the three poisons are like water bubbles, vainly appearing and vanishing.  
   Locus: `opening-shared-b`
3. `證實相 無人法 刹那滅却阿鼻業 若將妄語誑眾生 自招拔舌塵沙劫 頓覺了 如來禪 六度萬行體中圓`  
   English: Realize the true mark, and there is no person and no dharma. In a single instant the karma of Avici is extinguished. If I use false words to deceive living beings, I myself invite tongue-pulled hells for kalpas countless as dust. Once one is suddenly awakened to the Tathagata's Chan, the six perfections and the myriad practices are fulfilled within the body itself.  
   Locus: `opening-shared-c`
4. `夢裏明明有六趣 覺後空空無大千`  
   English: In a dream the six destinies seem vividly present; after awakening, even the great thousand-world system is empty.  
   Locus: `early-interior-shared-d`
5. `無罪福 無損益 寂滅性中莫問覓`  
   English: There is no sin or merit, no loss or gain. In the nature of quiescent extinction, do not ask or seek.  
   Locus: `early-interior-shared-e`
6. `比來塵鏡未曾磨 今日分明須剖析`  
   English: Until now the dust-covered mirror was never polished; today it must be clearly distinguished and laid open.  
   Locus: `early-interior-shared-f`
7. `誰無念 誰無生 若實無生無不生`  
   English: Who is without thought? Who is unborn? If one is truly unborn, then there is nothing not born from that.  
   Locus: `adjacent-interior-shared-g`
8. `喚取機關木人問 求佛施功早晚成`  
   English: Go ask the wooden puppet worked by its mechanism whether striving after buddhahood through applied effort will ever be accomplished.  
   Locus: `adjacent-interior-shared-h`
9. `放四大 莫把捉 寂滅性中隨飲啄`  
   English: Let go of the four great elements; do not clutch at them. Within the nature of quiescent extinction, simply follow your drinking and pecking.  
   Locus: `adjacent-interior-shared-i`
10. `諸行無常一切空 即是如來大圓覺`  
   English: All conditioned things are impermanent; all is empty. This itself is the Tathagata's great perfect awakening.  
   Locus: `adjacent-interior-shared-j`
11. `決定說 表真僧 有人不肯任情徵`  
   English: I speak decisively to show the true monk, yet there are people unwilling to let the evidence prove it according to the fact.  
   Locus: `adjacent-interior-shared-k`
12. `我心自空罪福無主 觀善惡如空華`  
   English: My mind is itself empty; sin and merit have no master. I view good and evil as flowers in the sky.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-l`
13. `智者無畏疑者懼 猶如虛空豈有涯`  
   English: The wise are fearless, the doubters afraid. How could empty space have any limit?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-m`
14. `還如木人看花鳥 何妨萬物假圍繞`  
   English: It is like a wooden man watching flowers and birds; what harm if the ten thousand things only seem to surround him?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-n`
15. `釋迦種裏收不得 卻羨神仙不肯要`  
   English: He cannot be contained within Shakya's lineage; still less would he envy the immortals.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-o`
16. `常獨行 常獨步 達者同遊涅槃路`  
   English: Always going alone, always walking alone: those who arrive travel together on the road to nirvana.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-p`
17. `調古神清風自高 貌悴骨剛人不顧`  
   English: His tune is ancient, his spirit clear, his bearing naturally lofty; his face is worn and his bones are hard, and people pay him no heed.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-q`
18. `窮釋子 口稱貧 實是身貧道不貧`  
   English: The poor son of Shakya speaks of poverty, but truly the body is poor while the Way is not poor.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-r`
19. `貧則身常披縷褐 道則心藏無價珍`  
   English: If poor, the body is always wrapped in ragged robes; but as for the Way, the mind stores a priceless jewel.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-s`
20. `無價珍 用無盡 利物應機終不吝`  
   English: That priceless jewel is used without exhaustion; in benefiting beings according to the occasion, it is never stingy.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-t`
21. `三身四智體中圓 八解六通心地印`  
   English: The three bodies and four wisdoms are complete within this body; the eight liberations and six superknowledges are the seal of the mind-ground.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-u`
22. `高低普應前後無差`  
   English: It responds universally to high and low; before and after, there is no difference.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-v`
23. `尋枝摘葉我不能也`  
   English: I cannot go hunting branches and plucking leaves.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-w`
24. `摩尼珠人不識 如來藏裏親收得`  
   English: People do not recognize the mani jewel; it is personally received within the Tathagata-garbha.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-x`
25. `六般神用空不空 一顆圓光色非色`  
   English: The six kinds of miraculous functioning are empty and not empty; one round radiance is form and not form.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-y`
26. `淨五眼 得五力 惟證乃知難可測`  
   English: Purify the five eyes, gain the five powers; only realization itself knows how hard they are to fathom.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-z`
27. `鏡裏看形見不難 水中捉月爭拈得`  
   English: Seeing a form in a mirror is not hard; who can snatch the moon in water and pick it up?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-aa`
28. `作麼生 行亦禪 坐亦禪`  
   English: What is it? Walking is Chan, sitting is Chan.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ab`
29. `語默動靜體安然`  
   English: In speech and silence, motion and stillness, the body is at peace.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ac`
30. `縱遇鋒刀常坦坦 假饒毒藥也閑閑`  
   English: Even when meeting naked blades, one remains always at ease; even if given poison, one stays utterly untroubled.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ad`
31. `我師得見燃燈佛 多劫曾為忍辱仙`  
   English: My teacher once saw Dipamkara Buddha and for many kalpas had been the patient immortal.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ae`
32. `幾回生 幾回死 生死悠悠無定止`  
   English: How many times born, how many times dead? Birth and death go on and on with no fixed end.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-af`
33. `自從頓悟了無生 於諸榮辱何憂喜`  
   English: Since suddenly awakening to the unborn, what worry or delight could there be over honor and disgrace?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ag`
34. `入深山 住蘭若 岑崟幽邃長松下`  
   English: Enter the deep mountains, dwell in a forest hermitage, beneath tall pines in craggy hidden depths.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ah`
35. `優游靜坐野僧家 闃寂安居實瀟灑`  
   English: At ease, sitting quietly in a wild monk's hut, dwelling in still seclusion, truly unrestrained.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ai`
36. `覺即了 不施功 一切有為法不同`  
   English: Awakening is itself completion; it does not apply effort. It is unlike every conditioned dharma.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-aj`
37. `住相布施生天福 猶如仰箭射虛空`  
   English: Giving while abiding in signs brings heavenly merit, but it is like shooting an arrow upward into empty space.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ak`
38. `勢力盡 箭還墜 招得來生不如意`  
   English: When the force is spent, the arrow falls back down, and one draws to oneself an unsatisfactory next life.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-al`
39. `爭似無為實相門 一超直入如來地`  
   English: How could that compare with the gate of the true mark of non-doing, where in one leap one enters directly the Tathagata-ground?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-am`
40. `但得本 莫愁末 如淨琉璃含寶月 既能解此如意珠 自利利他終不竭 江月照 松風吹 永夜清宵何所為`  
   English: Only get the root; do not worry about the branches. It is like pure lapis holding a precious moon. Once one can understand this wish-fulfilling jewel, benefiting oneself and others never comes to exhaustion. River-moon shining, pine-wind blowing, through the long night and clear evening, what is there to do?  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-an`
41. `佛性戒珠心地印 霧露雲霞體上衣 降龍鉢 解虎錫 兩鈷金環鳴歷歷 不是標形虛事持 如來寶杖親蹤跡`  
   English: The jewel-precept of buddha-nature is the seal on the mind-ground; mist, dew, clouds, and rosy haze are the robe upon the body. Dragon-subduing bowl, tiger-liberating staff, the two-pronged golden rings ring sharply. These are not empty accessories worn as marks of form; they are the dear traces of the Tathagata's precious staff.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ao`
42. `不求真 不斷妄 了知二法空無相 無相無空無不空 即是如來真實相 心鏡明 鑑無礙 廓然瑩徹周沙界`  
   English: Do not seek the true; do not cut off the false. Thoroughly know that both dharmas are empty and without marks. Without mark, without emptiness, with nothing not empty: this is the Tathagata's true real mark. The mind-mirror is bright, its reflection unobstructed; vast and limpid, it shines clear throughout worlds countless as sands.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ap`
43. `萬象森羅影現中 一顆圓光非內外 豁達空 撥因果 莽莽蕩蕩招殃禍 棄有著空病亦然 還如避溺而投火`  
   English: Myriad forms and densely arrayed phenomena appear within its reflections; the one round radiance is neither inside nor outside. To take emptiness in a wild and reckless way and reject cause and effect is to invite calamity. Abandoning being and clinging to emptiness is the same disease; it is like fleeing drowning only to throw oneself into fire.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-aq`
44. `捨妄心 取真理 取捨之心成巧僞 學人不了用修行 深成認賊將為子 損法財 滅功德 莫不由斯心意識 所以禪門了卻心 頓入無生知見力`  
   English: Cast aside the false mind and seize true principle: the mind of taking and rejecting becomes cunning falsity. Students who do not understand employ cultivation and deeply mistake a thief for their own son. To damage the wealth of dharma and destroy merit, all of it comes from this mind-consciousness. Therefore the Chan gate settles the mind completely and suddenly enters the power of the insight of the unborn.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ar`
45. `大丈夫 秉慧劍 般若鋒兮金剛焰 非但空摧外道心 早曾落却天魔膽 震法雷 擊法鼓 布慈雲兮灑甘露 龍象蹴蹋潤無邊 三乘五性皆醒悟`  
   English: The great man wields the sword of wisdom; the edge of prajna is a vajra flame. It not only shatters the minds of outsiders into emptiness, it long ago shattered the courage of heaven's demons. He shakes the thunder of dharma and beats the drum of dharma; he spreads clouds of compassion and sprinkles sweet dew. Dragon-elephants tread it down and the moisture reaches without bound; the three vehicles and five natures all awaken.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-as`
46. `雪山肥膩更無雜 純出醍醐我常納 一性圓通一切性 一法遍含一切法 一月普現一切水 一切水月一月攝 諸佛法身入我性 我性同共如來合 一地具足一切地 非色非心非行業 彈指圓成八萬門 剎那滅卻三祇劫 無量法門咸在目前 咫尺匪遙蹔時岐隔`  
   English: On Snow Mountain the rich cream is ever without admixture; the pure ghee I always take in. One nature perfectly penetrates all natures; one dharma pervades and contains all dharmas. One moon appears universally in every water; all water-moons are gathered in the one moon. The dharma-body of all buddhas enters my nature, and my nature together joins with the Tathagata. One ground fully contains every ground; it is neither form nor mind nor karmic activity. With a snap of the fingers the eighty-four thousand gates are rounded complete; in an instant the three asamkhyeya kalpas are extinguished. Immeasurable dharma-gates are all present before the eyes; they are no farther than an inch, though for the moment they seem divided.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-at`
47. `師子吼 無畏說 深嗟懵懂頑皮靼 只知犯重障菩提 不見如來開祕訣`  
   English: The lion's roar, the fearless teaching: I deeply lament the dull, thick-skinned fools. They know only that grave offenses obstruct bodhi; they do not see the Tathagata opening the secret key.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-au`
48. `有二比丘犯淫殺 波離螢光增罪結 維摩大士頓除疑 猶如赫日消霜雪`  
   English: There were two bhiksus who committed lust and murder. Upali's firefly light only increased the knot of guilt; the layman Vimalakirti at once removed their doubt, like a blazing sun melting frost and snow.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-av`
49. `不思議 解脫力 妙用恒沙也無極 四事供養敢辭勞 萬兩黃金亦銷得`  
   English: Inconceivable is the power of liberation; its wondrous function, constant as Ganges sands, is without limit. When the four requisites are offered, who would dare decline the toil? Even ten thousand taels of gold could be spent for it.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-aw`
50. `粉骨碎身未足酬 一句了然超百億 法中王 最高勝 河沙如來同共證`  
   English: Grinding bones to powder and shattering the body would still not repay it. One lucid phrase surpasses hundreds of billions. King within the dharma, highest and most excellent, it is jointly certified by Tathagatas numerous as river sands.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ax`
51. `我今解此如意珠 信受之者皆相應`  
   English: Now I understand this wish-fulfilling jewel; those who trustingly receive it all respond in accord.  
   Locus: `shared-interior-shared-ay`
