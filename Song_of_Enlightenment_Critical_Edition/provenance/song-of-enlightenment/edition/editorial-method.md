# Editorial Method: Song of Enlightenment

Date: 2026-05-15  
Status: active

## Edition model

This edition publishes a poem-first critical reading text of the `永嘉證道歌`.

- Base witness: `YJG-W22`
- Reading-text model: best-witness critical text
- Primary clean exact comparison witnesses: `YJG-W16`, `YJG-W17`
- Additional first-tier exact controls: `YJG-W2`, `YJG-W4C`, `YJG-W4F`, `YJG-W4G`, `YJG-W8`, `YJG-W9`
- Blocked exact witness: `YJG-W21`
- Commentary, reception, and derivative anthology witnesses: preserved as secondary controls, not promoted to equal reading-text authority

## Editorial rules

1. Do not silently normalize the poem to a fuller later tradition.
2. Accept a reading only where direct image evidence or already stabilized exact comparison evidence supports it.
3. Preserve the witness-level copy-text lock on `YJG-W22` while allowing cleaner shared-exact interiors in `YJG-W16` and `YJG-W17` to control adopted reading order and reading content where appropriate.
4. Record only material poem-level boundary, witness-class, closure, or blocked-source judgments in the selective apparatus.
5. Do not force commentary-bearing or derivative witnesses into ordinary line-by-line collation when their real value is witness-class or boundary support.

## Main reading-text posture carried into the edition

- The accepted poem body is now stabilized through `我今解此如意珠 信受之者皆相應`.
- `YJG-W22` is the selected copy-text at witness level because it is the earliest complete independent exact witness already held as a full local image tranche.
- `YJG-W16` and `YJG-W17` remain decisive clean comparison witnesses for much of the interior because their shared exact image surfaces are cleaner than the corresponding `YJG-W22` interior pages.
- The newly OCR-complete first-tier exact field (`YJG-W2`, `YJG-W4C`, `YJG-W4F`, `YJG-W4G`, `YJG-W8`, `YJG-W9`) strengthens opening, closure, and witness-class judgments but does not justify further adopted text change.

## Commentary handling

The package preserves commentary, reception, and derivative anthology material as a parallel secondary scholarly track.

They remain useful for:

- witness ecology
- packaging and boundary support
- later research and reception history
- intertextual or quotation tracking outside the main poem body

They do not control the main published reading text.

Japanese commentary is not part of the reader-facing edition logic for this work.
